PIPELINE_01

Race Video Intelligence

computer vision · built from scratch

race_intel/pipeline.py

model = YOLO("track_seg.pt")

for frame in stream.read(skip=cfg.frame_skip):
    masks = model.predict(frame, classes=TRACK_CLASSES)
    overlap = mask_overlap(masks.car, masks.track_limit)

    if overlap > cfg.violation_thresh:
        events.flag("track_limit_violation", frame.ts)

    tracker.update(masks.car)  # multi-frame overtake tracking
    if tracker.detect_overtake():
        events.flag("overtake", frame.ts)

what it does

  • YOLO-based segmentation of cars, track, curbs and walls, plus view-type classification.
  • Detects crashes, collisions and pit stops; proximity alerts and track-limit violations via mask-overlap analysis.
  • Multi-frame overtake tracking and HSV/Hough-Circle start-light detection, optimized with frame-skipping and parallel processing.
TECHNIQUE YOLO segmentation TRACKING multi-frame overtake detection OPTIMIZATION frame-skipping + parallel processing
